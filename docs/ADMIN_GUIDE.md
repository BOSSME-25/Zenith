# Zenith Admin Guide

A quick-start manual for managing zenithccprep.org.

---

## Start here: the rules that matter most

1. **The newsletter has no Publish button.** Tick **Published (visible on the site)** and click **Save issue**. That's the whole step. (Updates work the other way: a new update is published by default.)
2. **The Newsletter page leads with the highest issue number, not the newest save.** Give each issue a higher number than the last. An issue numbered lower than one already published goes to *Past issues*.
3. **Nothing goes on the Comets page without a signed release on file.** The Publish button stays greyed out until you record one. For anyone under 18, it must come from a parent or guardian.

> **Right now:** email notifications aren't switched on yet, so no form sends you an email. Check **Families, Contacts, Community, Partners, Nominations, and Feedback** in the admin yourself. Details are in section 9. *Delete this note once email is live.*

**If it's in the sidebar, it's yours to manage.** If it's words or layout on a page, send it to BOSS ME Technology at Zenith@bossmenow.com (section 13).

## 1. Logging in

Everyone has their own login: an email address and a password. Your name goes into the log next to what you post, so please don't share yours.

1. Go to **https://www.zenithccprep.org/admin**
2. Enter your **email** and your **password**.
3. The first time, you sign in with a **temporary password** that BOSS ME Technology gives you by phone or in person. The site asks you to **choose your own** before anything else opens. Use at least 10 characters. A few words together is easy to remember and hard to guess.
4. You'll land on your first page: the **Dashboard** for the school, **Meetings** for the board.

You'll stay signed in for about 7 days. Click **Sign out** in the bottom-left of the sidebar at any time. Change your password any time under **My account**.

**Who can open what.** The sidebar only shows what your role can use, and the site refuses the rest even if you type the address.

| **Board** | Lynn Palacios (Secretary), George Saad (Board President) | **Meetings**, **Site settings**, and **Feedback** |

> **If you forget your password,** or the site says your login is locked, email Zenith@bossmenow.com. A wrong password five times locks the login for 15 minutes. The developer can unlock it and give you a new temporary password.

### Feedback from your team

The **Admin Sign In** page has a **Share feedback or suggest a change** button under the sign-in box. It's for board members, faculty, and staff to suggest a change, send information you've asked for (a bio or a quote, say), correct something on the site, or report a problem. They don't need to be signed in, and an email address is optional.

- Everything they send appears under **Feedback** in the sidebar, newest first, tagged with the topic and the sender's role.
- Click **Mark done** once you've handled an item. It moves to the **Done** tab, and **Reopen** brings it back. **Delete** removes it permanently.
- If the sender left an email, it shows as a link so you can reply. Many won't leave one.
- Because anyone who finds the sign-in page can use it, treat what's there as suggestions, not instructions. The form asks people not to include students' names or personal details.
- You'll only get a notification email for new feedback once email is switched on (section 9). Until then, check the **Feedback** page yourself.

## 4. What you can do on each submissions page

On Families, Community, Partners, Contacts, and Subscribers you can:

- **Search**: type in the search box to filter the list
- **View detail**: every column is visible on the row
- **Export CSV**: click **Export CSV** in the top right. It downloads every entry in that section as a spreadsheet you can open in Excel or Google Sheets
- **Delete**: click the trash icon next to a row to remove it. Use sparingly; this is permanent

**A note on privacy:** the Families list contains children's names and grade levels, along with parents' contact details. Please don't share exports or screenshots outside the team, and delete downloaded files when you're done with them.

**A note on spam:** the Contacts list will occasionally collect unsolicited sales pitches (SEO services, marketing lists, and the like). They need no reply. Delete them.

## 5. Creating Updates (news posts)

Updates are public announcements that appear at **zenithccprep.org/updates** and are linked from the site header and footer.

### To create a new update

1. In the admin sidebar, click **Updates**
2. Click **New update** in the top right
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: what readers see (for example, "We submitted our charter application")
   - **Slug**: the web address path (for example, `charter-submitted`; keep it short, no spaces). The site generates one from the title and you can edit it
   - **Body**: the post content. Plain text or basic Markdown (`**bold**`, `# Heading`). A blank line starts a new paragraph
   - **Publish date**: defaults to today
   - **Published**: **ticked by default**, so the post goes live as soon as you save. Untick it to save a draft
4. Click **Publish update**

The post is live at `zenithccprep.org/updates/your-slug`.

> **There is no scheduling.** A published post goes live the moment you save it. The publish date only sets the date shown on the post and the order posts appear in. Setting a future date does not hold it back.

### To edit or hide an existing update

1. Go to **Updates** in the sidebar
2. Click **Edit** on the row you want to change
3. Make your changes (or untick **Published** to hide it)
4. Click **Save changes**

### Adding pictures to a post

1. While writing or editing, click in the **Body** where you want the photo to appear
2. Click the **Add image** button (just above the Body box)
3. Choose a photo from your computer (JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF, up to 10 MB)
4. It uploads and is inserted automatically as `![caption](link)`. Leave that text as-is; it becomes the picture on the published page
5. The text inside the square brackets (the file name by default) shows as a small caption under the photo. You can edit it to whatever caption you like
6. Click **Publish update** (or **Save changes**). The photo appears full-width on the live page

You can add several images to one post. Just repeat the steps for each.

### Good update topics

- Charter application milestones
- Community engagement moments ("Visited Arizona Educational Foundation today")
- Board appointments or partnership announcements
- Calls to action ("Survey now available in Spanish. Please share!")
- Behind-the-scenes from school design work

## 6. The Newsletter: "The Comet Trail"

The public **Newsletter** page (zenithccprep.org/newsletter) shows the latest published issue of The Comet Trail, with older issues in a **Past issues** archive below it. You write and edit issues entirely from the admin.

### Three things to know first

1. **There is no separate Publish button.** Tick **Published (visible on the site)** and click **Save issue**. That is the whole step.
2. **Issues are drafts by default.** The Published box starts unticked. If you forget it, the issue saves as a draft and nobody sees it. (This is different from Updates, which are published by default.)
3. **The public page leads with the highest issue number, not the newest save.** If you save Issue 2 while a published Issue 5 already exists, Issue 2 goes to Past issues and Issue 5 stays on top. Give each new issue a higher number than the last.

### Creating or editing an issue

1. In the admin sidebar, click **Newsletter**
2. Click **New issue** (or **Edit** next to an existing one)
3. Fill in the sections. Each one maps to a spot in the newsletter layout:
   - **Issue**: the issue number and a month label (for example, "September 2027")
   - **Top story**: lead photo, headline, a sentence or two, and an optional button
   - **From Dr. Samant**: a short, warm founder note
   - **Comet Spotlight**: a student or family photo, name, and a few sentences
   - **What's Happening**: up to three events with date, title, details, and link
   - **Inside the Classroom**: a short look at a project
   - **Big number**: one milestone, stated plainly (for example, "100%" and a sentence)
4. Any section you leave blank is simply hidden on the page, so you can't break the layout
5. To add a photo, click **Upload photo** in a section and choose an image from your computer
6. Tick **Published** if you want it live, then click **Save issue**

### After you save

You return to the issue list with a message that tells you exactly what happened:

- **"Issue 03 is live."** It has the highest number, so it leads the public page. A **View on the site** button opens it.
- **"Issue 03 is published, but it is not the lead issue."** A higher-numbered issue exists, so this one appears under Past issues. The message names the higher issue. To make it the lead, edit it and give it a higher number.
- **"Issue 03 saved as a draft."** It's visible only in the admin. Click Edit, tick **Published**, and save again.

In the list, a green **Published** badge means an issue is on the site, and a grey **Draft** badge means it isn't. The issue currently leading the public page also carries a dark **Showing as latest** badge. Drafts always appear in this list, so if an issue isn't here at all, it was never saved.

### If a save fails

While you write a new issue, the form backs up your work in your browser every couple of seconds. If a save fails, reopen **New issue** in the same browser and it offers to restore your text. Uploaded photos aren't part of the backup, so re-add them.

### Subscribers, and emailing the newsletter

People who enter their email on the Newsletter page are saved under **Subscribers** in the admin sidebar (search, export CSV, delete, same as the other lists). The same person signing up twice never creates a duplicate.

The website publishes the newsletter on the site and collects subscriber emails. It does **not** email the newsletter itself. To send an issue by email:

1. Click **Subscribers**, then **Export CSV**
2. Import the list into your email tool (Mailchimp, Constant Contact, and so on)
3. Send from there, and link readers to zenithccprep.org/newsletter

## 7. Comets: nominations and the recognition page

The **Comets** page (zenithccprep.org/alumni) recognizes students and graduates. It has two sections: **Comets in Motion** (current students reaching milestones such as dual enrollment or industry certifications) and **Comets Who've Landed** (graduates). Until the first class graduates in 2031, the second section shows a "coming soon" note.

Anyone can nominate a student or graduate at **zenithccprep.org/alumni/nominate**: students, staff, families, and community members. **Nothing is published automatically.** Every nomination comes to you first.

### Step 1: Review nominations

1. Click **Nominations** in the sidebar. New ones appear under **Pending**
2. Read the story, the relationship of the person nominating, and any contact details. The nominator's email is for follow-up only and is never published
3. Choose:
   - **Approve**: creates an *unpublished draft profile* and shows you a link to finish it under Comets
   - **Reject**: asks for a short reason. The reason stays internal and is never shown publicly

### Step 2: Finish the profile

1. Click **Comets** in the sidebar. Approved nominations appear there as drafts
2. Click **Edit** and complete the profile:
   - **Photo**, **headline** (the short line on the card), and **full story**
   - **Field tags** (for example, Healthcare, Skilled Trades), which power the "Browse by field" filter on the public page
   - **Certifications**, **grade or graduation year**, and the **milestone type**
   - **Section**: In Motion (currently enrolled) or Landed (graduated)
3. **Consent comes before publishing.** Tick **"A signed release is on file for this person"** and fill in **who signed, and when**. For anyone under 18, this must be a **parent or guardian**, not the student's own consent
4. Click **Save profile**

### Step 3: Publish

Back on the Comets list, click **Publish**. **The Publish button stays greyed out until a signed release is on file.** The site itself also refuses to publish without one, so this isn't just a button. If you later untick the release, the profile comes down immediately.

To take a profile off the public page at any time, click **Unpublish**.

### Mentors

Ticking **Available as a mentor** on a profile adds a "Connect with" button to that person's card. Messages sent through it are emailed to the school, never to the person directly, and the person's own contact details are never shown.

> **Mentor messages need email set up** (see section 9). Until then, leave this unticked: a message sent through the button would be lost.

## 11. Quick troubleshooting

**"Application error" on /admin**
The database may be temporarily unreachable. Wait a minute and reload. If it persists, email Zenith@bossmenow.com.

**Login keeps failing**
- Check Caps Lock
- Make sure the email and password match exactly (no leading or trailing space)
- If you copied it from a message, retype it manually
- Five wrong passwords lock the login for 15 minutes. Email Zenith@bossmenow.com to have it unlocked.

**I saved a newsletter issue but it isn't at the top of the Newsletter page**
Look at the issue list. If it says **Draft**, edit it and tick **Published**. If it says **Published** but another issue is marked **Showing as latest**, that other issue has a higher number. Edit yours and give it a higher number than that one.

**A newsletter issue seems saved but I can't find it**
Drafts and published issues both appear in the Newsletter list. If it isn't there, the save didn't go through. Reopen **New issue** in the same browser and restore your backed-up draft.

**An update I published isn't showing on /updates**
- Confirm **Published** is ticked
- Hard-refresh the public page (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows)

**The Publish button on a Comet profile is greyed out**
A signed release isn't on file. Click **Edit**, tick **"A signed release is on file"**, add who signed and when, and save. Publish then becomes available.

**At an event, a family sees "submitted this form several times recently"**
The forms limit how often a single internet connection can submit, to block spam. The interest list allows 100 per hour per connection, Community allows 20, and Contact and Partner allow 10. The interest list is set high on purpose so a busy tabling event doesn't turn families away, so you're unlikely to see this there. If someone does, switch them to phone data or wait a few minutes.

**A form says thank you but I never got an email**
Check the admin list. The submission is saved even when email isn't (see section 9).

**CSV export downloads an empty file**
That section genuinely has no submissions yet. It isn't an error.

## 12. Public meetings (for the Secretary)

Zenith's board meets in public, and Arizona law requires a notice and agenda to be posted at least 24 hours before each meeting (A.R.S. § 38-431.02). This section of the admin puts the notice on the public **Public Meetings** page, keeps every agenda and set of minutes in one place, and records when each thing was posted. You never edit code or ask the web team.

The system enforces the 24-hour rule itself. If a notice is too close to the meeting, it will not publish, and it tells you to call the Board Chair.

### One-time setup (Site settings)

1. **Where printed notices are posted.** The notice has to name a physical place as well as the website. The board designates the place by vote and records it in its minutes. Enter it only after that vote. Until then the page names the website only, and a red **Notice location not set** banner shows in the admin.
2. **Accommodation requests** (optional, the board decides). Name or role, email or phone, and how many business days ahead.
3. **Yearly meeting schedule.** Each August, upload the year's schedule as a PDF. That is the only yearly task. The archive groups itself by school year.

Your name and title are recorded automatically from your login, next to a time the server records, so there is nothing to choose or type. Two people have board logins (the Secretary and the Board President), so a notice never depends on one person being away.

### Post an upcoming meeting

Do this at least 72 hours ahead if you can. Twenty-four hours is the legal minimum, not the target.

1. Click **Meetings**, then **New meeting**.
2. Choose the type. **Regular** is the standard monthly meeting.
3. Enter the title. Pick the **Date**, then choose the **Starts** time (and **Ends**, if you want one) from the lists. The line under them shows exactly how the public page will read, so check it. Under **How will people attend?** choose **Online only**, **In person only**, or **In person and online**, and the form shows just the fields that apply. For an online meeting, paste the Zoom link and add the meeting ID, passcode, and dial-in. The public page shows "Online - Zoom" for a Zoom link. **Times are Arizona time. Do not convert anything.**
4. Click **Save as draft**. Nothing is public yet.
5. On the next screen, under **Agenda**, upload the agenda as a PDF. If it is not ready, come back later. Do not publish without it unless the meeting is more than 48 hours away.
6. Under **Notice**, click **Publish notice**. A box asks you to confirm the date and time; read it before you say yes. The system records the date and time. If you see a red message about 24 hours, stop and call the Board Chair.
7. Open the public page (the link is on the meeting) and check the date, time, place, and agenda link.
8. Print the agenda and post it at the physical location named in the notice. The law needs both places.

### After the meeting: minutes

1. Within three working days, open the meeting and go to **Minutes**.
2. Upload the draft minutes as a PDF and choose **Draft, pending board approval**. The page labels them that way.
3. **Never upload executive session minutes or materials.** They stay confidential.
4. When the board approves the minutes, open the same meeting, upload the approved PDF, choose **Approved by the board**, and enter the approval date. It replaces the draft; the draft stays on record.

### Cancel, move, or correct

- **Cancel:** open the meeting, type the reason, and click **Cancel meeting**. The page keeps the row and shows "Cancelled" with your reason. Take down the printed notice and post a cancellation notice.
- **Move:** create and publish the meeting for the new date first. Then open the old one, pick the new meeting, and click **Move meeting**. The old row reads "Moved to [date]". Replace the printed notice.
- **Change the time or place of a posted meeting:** edit it under **Details**. The same 24-hour rule applies, and the change is logged.
- **Wrong file:** upload the right one with **Replace**, or use **Withdraw this file** under **Files on record** and give a reason.
- **Nothing is ever deleted.** A replaced or withdrawn file stays in storage and in the log. The only thing you can remove is a draft that was never posted.

### Past meetings

To add meetings that already happened, choose **New meeting**, tick **This meeting already happened**, and attach the agenda and approved minutes. No notice is posted.

### What the tabs and badges mean

- **Upcoming:** drafts and posted meetings that have not happened yet. A red **Agenda missing** badge means a posted meeting is within 48 hours and has no agenda.
- **Needs minutes:** meetings that have happened without approved minutes. A red **Minutes overdue** badge means three working days have passed with no minutes uploaded.
- **Archive:** everything else.

### The enrollment pop-up

Under **Site settings**, the **Open enrollment pop-up** box controls a pop-up that appears on public pages shortly after they load, once per visit. It is **Hidden** by default and shows nothing. Now that Zenith is approved, **Coming soon** is the right choice: it points people to the interest list. Switch to **Open** once the application link works, and put that link in the button. The suggested wording is a draft: Jay and the board approve every word, and every claim has to match the charter terms. It never appears on the interest list page.
